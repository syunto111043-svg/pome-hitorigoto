About / サービス概要

pome-hitorigoto is a personal desktop tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @pome_hitorigoto to post the owner's own original short videos (career and recruiting tips) to the owner's own TikTok account.

  • Users: the channel owner only. The tool is not offered, sold or shared with anyone else.
  • TikTok features: Login Kit (the owner signs in to their own account) and Content Posting API (upload / post videos to the owner's own account).
  • Before posting, the owner reviews the title and chooses the privacy level and interaction settings. Videos containing AI-generated material are labelled as AI-generated content.

「ポメのひとりごとch」が自社チャンネル（@pome_hitorigoto）に、自ら制作した採用・キャリア系のオリジナルショート動画を投稿するための個人用ツールです。利用者は運営者本人のみで、第三者への提供・販売はしません。
